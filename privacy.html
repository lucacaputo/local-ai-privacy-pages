Local AI

Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Who runs Local AI

Local AI is software installed on a private Windows PC in a home or a small office. Each installation is run by the person who installed it (the "operator"), with its own Google Cloud project and its own Google sign-in app. The authors of Local AI run no online service, and they receive no data from any installation.

What Google data the assistant can use

Only after you sign in with Google and agree on Google's consent screen, the assistant can use:

  • your name and email address, to know which account it is working with;
  • Gmail: reading, searching and labelling your mail, and preparing drafts. Sending mail needs your confirmation;
  • Google Calendar: reading and changing your events;
  • Google Drive, Docs and Sheets: finding, reading, creating and editing your files.

How it is used

Your Google data is used only to carry out the tasks you ask the assistant to do. It is processed on the installation's PC by AI models that run on that same PC, and it is not sent to any other AI service. The assistant does not send mail, delete anything or share files without your confirmation.

Where it is kept

The Google sign-in is stored on the installation's PC only, in Windows Credential Manager and in the assistant's private storage. Data the assistant reads from your account stays on that PC. It is kept only as long as the task needs it, or as long as you keep the files the assistant made for you. The operator can delete all of it.

What is never done with it

Your Google data is not sold. It is not shared with anyone, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models.

Local AI's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Stopping access

You can remove the assistant's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by asking the operator to sign you out. After that the assistant can no longer reach your account.

Contact

Ask the operator of your installation: their address is on the Google sign-in screen. For this policy: ercapperones@gmail.com.
