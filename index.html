Local AI

A private AI assistant that runs on your own computer.

Local AI is software that a household or a small office installs on its own Windows PC. The assistant and the AI models it uses run on that PC. People in the household reach it from their own devices over a private network.

Google sign-in

You can let the assistant work with your Google account: read and organise your mail, prepare replies, look at and update your calendar, and work on your Drive files, Docs and Sheets. It acts only when you ask it to. It does not send mail, delete anything or share files without your confirmation.

Every Local AI installation signs in to Google with its own app, set up by whoever installed it. The Google sign-in screen shows "Local AI" and that person's contact address.
